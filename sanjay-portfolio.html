About Me

first language: charcoal
manifesto
Studio note
not a tool-chaser

I started with my hands. Charcoal, a blank page, and something I had to say. That instinct — the need to make something that lands — hasn't left me. It just grew. Now I design event campaigns seen by thousands, lead a creative community at one of India's best technical institutions, and figure out what AI can do when it's held by someone who actually understands visual craft. I'm not a tool-chaser. I'm building taste that makes every tool sharper. At IIT Kanpur, I lead the Fine Arts Club, I've competed nationally in art, and I've worn multiple creative hats — event branding, merch design, startup marketing, motion and AI video. I'm looking for the place where beautiful work and real-world impact are the same goal.
